# Second reviewer: how to fill the sheet (about 1 minute per row, 50 rows)
Open `review_sheet_reviewer2.csv` in Excel or Google Sheets. Work alone: do NOT look at anyone else's sheet or ask what they marked.
For each row read the passage first, then the three questions. Answer yes or no only:
- **bangla_natural / mixed_natural**: would a real person in Bangladesh write this question this way?
- **bangla_answerable / mixed_answerable**: does THIS passage contain the answer?
- **romanized_natural_as_written**: is the Romanized question fine as it is (spelling and wording like real chat)? If no, type the corrected question in `romanized_corrected_text_if_no`.
- **romanized_answerable**: does the passage answer it, assuming you can understand the Romanized words?
- **same_meaning_all_three**: do the Bangla, mixed and Romanized questions ask the same thing?
- **notes**: anything unclear (a wrong fact, two possible answers, a word you could not read).
Be strict: "no" is a normal answer. Put your initials in `reviewer_initials`.
