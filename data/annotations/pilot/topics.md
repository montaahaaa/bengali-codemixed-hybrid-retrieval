# Pilot topic list (phase 2.1)

Six domains, 8 topics each, in Bangladesh context. Topics are neutral descriptions only. No example queries and no wording that a writer could copy (used as elicitation prompts in 2.4 and as mining seeds in 2.2).

## Fees
1. Passport application and renewal charges
2. Income tax return filing cost and deadlines
3. Mobile banking transaction charges
4. Bank account opening and maintenance charges
5. University and college admission fees
6. Visa application fees
7. Driving licence and vehicle registration fees
8. Utility connection and late-payment charges

## Procedures
1. Applying for or correcting a national ID card
2. Birth and death registration
3. Applying for a driving licence
4. Applying for a visa to travel abroad
5. Opening a bank account
6. Registering a SIM card or recovering a lost one
7. Land mutation and property registration
8. Getting a trade licence for a small business

## Health
1. Fever, cold and common infections: care at home
2. Diabetes and blood pressure management
3. Child vaccination schedule
4. Pregnancy and newborn care
5. Dengue prevention and symptoms
6. When to see a doctor or go to hospital
7. Diet, nutrition and exercise
8. Mental health and stress

## Education
1. Admission after SSC and HSC
2. Checking exam results and board procedures
3. Scholarships for study at home and abroad
4. Studying abroad: tests and requirements
5. Choosing a subject or career path
6. Online courses and learning platforms
7. Job exams and public service recruitment
8. Learning English

## Tech
1. Forgotten phone or account password
2. Slow Wi-Fi and internet problems
3. Protecting against viruses and scams
4. Mobile phone settings and storage
5. Setting up and using email
6. Installing and updating software
7. Online payment safety
8. Basic programming and freelancing skills

## Local services
1. Train ticket booking and refunds
2. Bus and metro travel in the city
3. Electricity bill payment and meter problems
4. Gas and water supply complaints
5. Ride-sharing and local transport
6. Postal and courier services
7. Hospital and clinic appointments
8. Emergency numbers and complaint channels
